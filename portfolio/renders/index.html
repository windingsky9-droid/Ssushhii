What these are, and what they aren't

  • No alpha channel. Finished studio scenes — backdrop, ground plane and contact shadow baked in. Not cut-outs.
  • Not editable mockups. No PSD, no smart object. The white band on the bottle is geometry, not a placeholder for a label.
  • Not real products. ORBIT ONE, SERUM 30ML, ARC TABLE LAMP, FORM 62 and MONITOR 5 are invented for this set.
  • Not photorealistic. Analytic lighting, not a captured HDR environment. It reads as a clean product render — which is the point.
