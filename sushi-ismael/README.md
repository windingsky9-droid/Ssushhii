# Sushi & Ismael

An interactive 3D binary star built in the same visual language as the earlier Sushi & Ez build: Syncopate and IBM Plex Mono, Sushi in ice-blue and lime, the partner star in gold and rose, and a lilac bridge between them.

Open `index.html` in a current browser. It loads three.js 0.160 and Google Fonts from their CDNs, so it needs a connection.

## What is in the scene

- **Two stars on a real Kepler orbit.** Equal mass (q = 1.00), eccentricity 0.12, a 44-second period. They speed up near periastron, and the telemetry panel reads out separation, phase and speed live.
- **Animated star surfaces.** Granulation, sunspots and limb darkening come from 3D simplex noise, with a two-tone corona: lime edges on Sushi and rose edges on Ismael.
- **The bond.** A double helix of particles runs between the stars. The blue strand flows toward Ismael and the gold strand flows toward Sushi, and pulses travel along the base-pair rungs.
- **Lit by both stars.** Three planets, a ringed ice giant among them, carry both colors, blue on one side and gold on the other. The dust belts shift color the same way as the stars move around them.
- **Astrolabe ring.** Degree ticks, with both names engraved around the orbital plane.
- **Names in the sky.** SUSHI and ISMAEL are drawn as constellations: 11 letters made of 105 stars, which ignite letter by letter.
- **Memories.** Type a memory and it rises out of the barycenter into its own orbit. Memories are kept in the viewer's browser only.
- **Sound (optional).** The two stars sound a perfect fifth apart (A3 and E4). Each tone pans with its star across the screen and swells as the stars draw close.

## Controls

- Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom.
- Click a star to fly to it.
- Keys `1`–`5` jump between chapters and `T` toggles the guided tour.

No birth data or private details are included; the page shows first names only.
