# Low-Cost Money-Building Stack

Goal: use AI/plugins to build and sell something useful, rather than paying for many subscriptions before revenue exists.

## Phase 0 - validate at nearly $0/month

| Tool | Cost starting point | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| GitHub | $0 Free | Source control, portfolio, issues, CI |
| Factor Weave | $0 Free | REST market research, 250 calls/day |
| Supabase | $0 Free | Database/auth for prototypes |
| Resend | $0 Free | Up to 3,000 emails/month |
| Stripe | $0 monthly | Payments; standard US cards are usage-priced |
| Apollo | $0 Free | Internal prospect research/outreach |
| vidIQ | $0 Free | YouTube ideas and channel growth research |

Do not buy everything at once. First prove that people want the product or service.

## Phase 1 - first commercial version

Recommended core spend: about **$39/month before payment fees**.

- Factor Weave Hobby: $19/month for MCP and 2,500 API calls/day.
- Vercel Pro: $20/month for professional/commercial deployment.
- Keep Supabase and Resend on Free until limits become a real constraint.
- Stripe stays usage-priced, so cost follows actual sales.

## Phase 2 - only after revenue

- Supabase Pro: from $25/month when production database limits justify it.
- Resend Pro: $20/month for 50,000 emails/month.
- Apollo Basic: about $49/user/month on annual billing if outbound sales is converting.
- Factor Weave Pro: $79/month when you need 25,000 calls/day, forward-return labels, label-aware similarity, or risk-cluster tags.
- Semrush SEO: $139/month monthly, or $117.33/month billed annually, only when SEO is a proven acquisition channel.

## Best first products

1. Factor-based stock research dashboard with clear research-only framing.
2. Daily watchlist/report-card email with configurable alerts.
3. Educational market screener for creators or communities.
4. Custom research dashboard service for small businesses or creators.
5. Paid newsletter that combines market context with human commentary.

## Guardrails

- Factor Weave itself says similarity is a screening lens, not a forward-return prediction service.
- Never commit API keys, passwords, or Stripe secrets to GitHub.
- Verify Factor Weave commercial/data-redistribution terms before charging customers for raw provider data.
- Apollo standard plans are for internal business use; external resale/data-powered products require separate terms.
- Prefer recurring software/service revenue over speculative trading as the main wealth-building engine.
